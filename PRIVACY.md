# Privacy

Adds Up is a set of instructions and one Python script that run inside your own Claude session. It has no server, no account and no connector.

## What the plugin does with your documents

When you ask Claude to check a file, Claude runs the bundled script on that file. The script reads the file, prints its text, tables and formulas into the conversation, and exits. It writes no file, keeps no copy, makes no network request and contains no analytics or tracking.

## What the author receives

Nothing. The plugin sends no data to its author or to any third party, and the author has no way to see which files you check or what they contain.

## What Claude does with your documents

The content of a file you check becomes part of your conversation with Claude, like any other file you share with it. How that conversation is handled is governed by your own agreement with Anthropic and by the privacy settings of your Claude account, not by this plugin.

## Questions

Open an issue at https://github.com/Aphro4266/adds-up/issues. Please do not paste confidential document content into a public issue.
